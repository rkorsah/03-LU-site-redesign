About Us

Rich's Flower Garden was founded in 2026 by Desmond Korsah to share joy, beauty, and connection with the Mission District of New Jersey. We remain rooted in that vision, brightening lives with every bouquet while supporting our community. Ten percent of each sale continues to fund efforts to fight homelessness across the Garden State.

Thank you for helping us grow kindness one flower at a time.
